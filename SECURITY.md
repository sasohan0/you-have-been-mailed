# Security Policy

## 🔒 Our Commitment to Privacy & Security

**You Have Been Mailed** is designed from the ground up to be **100% self-hosted and private**:
- **Zero Third-Party Servers**: No telemetry, analytics, or external tracking servers are ever contacted.
- **Personal Google Cloud**: All tracking data, timestamps, recipient logs, and email replies reside exclusively inside your own personal Google Account (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script container).
- **Client-Side Manifest V3**: Strict Content Security Policy (CSP) compliant, zero external scripts or CDNs loaded.

---

## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover any security or privacy vulnerability within this project, please do not open a public issue.

Instead, please email the maintainer privately:
- **Email**: [sasohan0@gmail.com](mailto:sasohan0@gmail.com)
- Subject: `[Security] You Have Been Mailed Vulnerability Report`

Please include:
1. Type and description of vulnerability
2. Step-by-step instructions to reproduce
3. Potential impact

We take all reports seriously and will respond within 48 hours to coordinate a fix and release.
